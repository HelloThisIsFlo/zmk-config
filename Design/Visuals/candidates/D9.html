why? (the mechanism in two sentences)

Real two-key combos fire on presses up to 15 ms apart (M+W is Z). A roll under 10 ms sits inside that range, so giving it up is the intended trade. 11–25 ms is where the inconsistency was: 11 pairs used to be unreliable there.

⏸ at exactly 10 ms and the ⚖️ edges are hardware predictions. 11 pairs behave like kept (the 2nd key has no combo: X C F and a thumb), 34 like column; together 45 pairs with no shared combo. The 6 pairs that share a real combo and the 3 with no timing band type the same with the hack on or off. Numbers from Design/Timing/FACTS.md.
