# T-16 — hold-tap forced TAP after an `&ak` within 9 ms (quick-tap skew) + control Δ = 12

> Rig R1 (hack ON, template unchanged). Key 1 = pos 1 `&ak 2 B` (hack-only), key 2 = pos 9 `&lk X Y` (linger hold-tap, tap-preferred 100, tap `&bk Y` 0x1C / hold `&bk X` 0x1B, hack-only; `&trans` on akB).
> Four cases, one directory each under `zmk/app/tests/ak-truth/`: `T-16-a-d6` (spec), `T-16-b-d12` (spec control), `T-16-c-d10` + `T-16-d-d11` (**extra** boundary probes, not in the spec).
> Raw logs + snapshots: `results/T-16-{a-d6,b-d12,c-d10,d-d11}.raw.log` / `.snapshot`. Case generator: `results/T-16.mkcases.py`.
> All four built and ran (`PASS` after auto-accept); a second run of each `PASS`es against its snapshot (deterministic).

## 🎯 Verdict: **deviates** (mechanism confirmed; two spec timings wrong)

- ✅ **Forced TAP confirmed.** Linger key 6 ms after an `&ak` → at `t+12`: `new undecided hold_tap` then `decided tap (tap-preferred decision moment quick-tap)` in the same ms. Held 312 ms and still Y. **No X (0x1B) anywhere in the log.**
- ✅ **Control confirmed.** Δ = 12 → `decided hold-timer (tap-preferred decision moment timer)` → X. No Y.
- ❌ **Y's HID↓ is at `t+23`, not `t+12`.** The TAP decision is at `t+12`, but `&bk Y` goes into the behaviour queue behind B's `&ak` macro. It is emitted after B↑ and `layer 2 state 1`, all at `[t+23]`.
- ❌ **Control X↓ is at `t+113`, not `t+112`.** The hold-tap term runs from the linger key's own timestamp: `t+12 + 100 = t+112`. native_posix fires it 1 ms late, as it does every timer.
- 🔬 **Native boundary from the extras.** Δ = 10 → forced TAP (Y). Δ = 11 → normal HOLD (X at `t+112`). This matches MODEL: "Δ ≤ 10 on native_posix is all bypass". On the board the band is Δ ≤ 9.
- 🆕 **Side fact.** In every case, the linger output's own key-up (Y↑ or X↑) is what switches the akB sticky layer OFF. With forced TAP, akB is alive only from `t+23` to `t+34`.
