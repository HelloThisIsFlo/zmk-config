# T-20 — Held modifier pins the AK layer past 100 ms (+ ignore-modifiers control)

> **Verdict: `as_predicted`.** Every spec line holds on native_posix.
> - A Shift pressed after the AK letter keeps layer 1 up past `t+129`. The layer drops only in the same ms as `kp_released 0xE1`.
> - With `ignore-modifiers` on `aksl`, the layer drops at `t+129` and the follower types B.
> - One 1-ms note: K lands at **t+363**, not "~t+362". It comes from the B-key release, which the mock puts at 352 + 10 + 1.
>
> Three extra sub-cases sharpen MODEL (c) "Consumption":
> - a sticky Shift (`skms`) also pins the layer, but only until the next letter's key-up;
> - a Shift held *before* the AK letter does **not** pin;
> - while a held Shift pins the layer, a **second** follower tap is adapted too.

## 📋 Spec (CLAIMS.md, quoted)

> **T-20 Modifier pins the AK layer** — R1 with pos 11 = `&kp LSHIFT` for this test. `PRESS(0,0,50) PRESS(2,3,300) PRESS(0,1,10) RELEASE(0,1,50) RELEASE(2,3,10) RELEASE(0,0,300)`: expect `layer 1 state 1` at `t+28`, **no** `layer 1 state 0` at `t+129`, `kp_pressed 0x0E` (K, adapted, shifted) at ~`t+362`, `layer 1 state 0` only after `kp_released 0xE1`. Control with `ignore-modifiers` on `aksl` → `state 0` at `t+129`, B typed. Purpose: E-60 nuance.

- **Model under test:** MODEL.md (c), "Consumption (PROVEN, `sk:268-310`)". Quote:
  > the first keycode DOWN after arming stops the 100 ms timer and pins the layer until that keycode's UP (after HID processed it); modifiers count (no `ignore-modifiers`), so a held/sticky Shift pins the AK layer until Shift up (≤ 1500 ms for `skms`)
- **Claims touched:**
  - CLAIMS E-60 ("pinned by any keycode down incl. modifiers")
  - CLAIMS summary point 16 ("pinned by any held modifier")

### 🔧 Rig corrections (spec vs RIG.md)

- **akA pos 11 had to become `&trans`.**
  - The template's layer 1 (akA) binds pos 11 to `&bk M`.
  - The Shift press arrives while layer 1 is up (t+51 > t+28), so it would resolve to `&bk M` and type an M, not a Shift.
  - Every case with `&kp LSHIFT` on pos 11 therefore also sets akA pos 11 = `&trans`. The log then shows `layer_id: 1 … transparent` → `layer_id: 0 … key_press`.
- **Shift is combo-delayed.** Pos 11 sits in `V16 <6 11>` + the hack, so Shift is delivered at press + 17 (t+68), not at t+51. This doesn't matter for the test: t+68 is still inside the window.
- **Times (RIG rule: first event's ms is doubled, every gap is ms+1):**

  | Event | abs ms | rel |
  |---|---|---|
  | A↓ | 51 | t |
  | Shift↓ | 102 | t+51 |
  | B-key↓ | 403 | t+352 |
  | B-key↑ | 414 | t+363 |
  | Shift↑ | 465 | t+414 |
  | A↑ | 476 | t+425 |
  | exit | 777 | t+726 |

- **"Shifted" is not in the K line itself.**
  - The `hid_listener_keycode_pressed` line prints the *event's* own `explicit_mods`, which is 0x00 for K.
  - Shift in the report shows in `zmk_hid_implicit_modifiers_press: Modifiers set to 0x02` in the same ms.
  - Shift's hold lasts from `zmk_hid_register_mod: Modifier 1 count 1` to `Modifier 1 released`.

## 🔩 Board translation (nRF52840, MODEL §(a))

- **Remove native's +1s.** Each timer hop is +1 on native and `+δ` (0.03–1.05 ms) on the board. Mock gaps are `ms+1` on native. On the board they're simply the finger's timing, plus the ≈5 ms debounce that every physical edge gets equally.
- **Timer-driven instants (board, relative to A's engine timestamp T):**

  | Event | native | board |
  |---|---|---|
  | A↓ | T+17 | T+16+δ |
  | layer 1 ON | T+28 | T+26+2δ |
  | unpinned OFF (b, d) | T+129 | ≈T+126 + 3δ |
  | Shift (`&kp` on a 16-ms-combo position) delivered | T_S+17 | T_S+16+δ |
  | `htls` hold-tap created (hack-only thumb) | T_thumb+12 | T_thumb+11+δ |

- **Instants with no timer are the same on board.** Relative to their physical edges:
  - B-key flush at B-key↑;
  - a: layer OFF at Shift↑;
  - c: layer OFF at K↑ (the queue tick is K_MSEC(10), so +δ);
  - c: tap decision at thumb↑.
- **Pin condition on the board.**
  - The modifier's keycode DOWN must be *delivered* before the sticky timer fires (≈ `D_A + 110 + δ`). Delivery comes after the modifier's own combo wait, or after the hold-tap decision for `htls`.
  - The extra guard `ev.timestamp > release_at` (`behavior_sticky_key.c`, key-down branch) compares the keycode's *original* timestamp: the position timestamp for a raw `&kp`, the thumb-press timestamp for an `htls` tap (`behavior_hold_tap.c:402-436`). That guard is always looser than the delivery condition.
  - A modifier pressed less than its own combo wait (11–18 ms) before the window closes arrives too late to pin. At the exact boundary, the board's δ decides.
- **Nothing observed here would flip on the board.** None of the five cases sits within 2 ms of the t+129 boundary: the nearest pinning event is c's Shift at t+103.

## 🧠 What this settles

- ✅ **MODEL (c) Consumption holds as written**, including "after HID processed it": `kp_released 0xE1` → unregister mod → `mo` release → layer OFF, all in one ms.
  - a: held Shift pins until Shift↑.
  - c: sticky `skms` Shift pins until the sticky Shift itself releases, which is the next letter's key-up.
  - b: `ignore-modifiers` removes the pin.
- ⚠️ **"Pinned by any held modifier" (CLAIMS point 16 / E-60 reason) is too broad.** Only a modifier whose DOWN arrives *after* the `aksl` is armed pins the layer (d). A modifier already held when the AK letter is typed changes nothing. MODEL's own wording ("the first keycode DOWN after arming") is exact.
- 🆕 **A pinned layer adapts every key until the modifier's UP (e), not just the first one.**
  - With a held `&kp` modifier this could run for any length of time.
  - With `skms` it ends at the first letter (c), so in Flo's LH1 sticky-shift path at most one extra letter is affected.
  - *INFERRED, not tested here:* in Flo's real keymap, any held `htm` home-row mod (hold-while-undecided, `l_mod`) pressed within the window would pin the same way until released.
