# 🧪 T-17: `htls` hold-preferred (interrupted, tapped alone, held alone)

> **Status: complete.** 8 sub-cases built and ran on 2026-09-30: the 3 spec cases (a) (b) (c), plus 5 extras.
> - Extras: (a2) no-combo control, (a3)/(a4) the same shape on Flo's real keymap, (b2) a long tail to see the sticky expire, (d) a key buffered together with the thumb.
> - Timestamped logs come from `zmk.exe --no-rt`. A second `run-test.sh` run of (a) without auto-accept PASSes against its snapshot, so the output is deterministic.
>
> **Verdict: `deviates` (one line in (a)).** Every `htls` claim holds: hold-preferred interrupt → HOLD, lone tap → sticky LSHIFT at key-up, lone hold → timer at t+90 (+1 native), not t+102.
> - The deviation: in the spec rig (a), `kp_pressed 0xE0` (LCTRL) comes at **t+50**, not t+31.
> - Why: the interrupting key is released from the hold-tap at t+31 and then goes through the combo engine on the **mod layer**. There, R1's `l_all` tier combos S16/Q18 hold it for 18 ms (+1 native).
> - Controls: with no combo on that key on the mod layer, LCTRL comes at t+31, both in R1 (a2) and on the real keymap with LM4 (a3). The real LM1 (`entr`, layers 0-31) behaves like R1 pos 5: LSHIFT at t+48 (a4).

## 🎯 Result first

- ✅ **Interrupt → HOLD immediately (E-100 confirmed, contradicting "tap < 90 ms = sticky shift").**
  - In every interrupt run, the other key's press decides `hold-interrupt` in the same ms (t+31). `&mo` of the mod layer runs synchronously through the idle queue, so `layer … state 1` is logged in that same ms.
- ❌ **The htm mod is not always down in the decision ms.**
  - The re-raised interrupting key is subject to combos active on the mod layer: `l_all` combos in R1 and in the real keymap. The hack is not one of them, because it is `l_alpha_aks` only.
  - No such combo on that key: LCTRL at t+31 (a2, a3).
  - An `l_all` combo on that key: mod at t+31 + longest candidate + 1 (R1 pos 5: +19 → t+50; real LM1 in `entr`: +17 → t+48).
- ✅ **Tap alone → `decided tap (hold-preferred decision moment key-up)` at t+51 → sticky LSHIFT 0xE1** in the same ms.
- ✅ **Held alone → `decided hold-timer (hold-preferred decision moment timer)` at [t+91] = t+90 + 1 native.**
  - The hack flush at t+12 does not extend the term, which is counted from the original timestamp.
  - Nothing happens between the hold-tap's creation at t+12 and t+91: no layer, no HID. So `htls` has no hold-while-undecided (E-99/E-108 hold).
- 🆕 **New facts:**
  - **Sticky LSHIFT expires 1500 ms after the THUMB PRESS, not after the tap.** `skms` `release_at = event.timestamp + 1500`, and the hold-tap passes its original timestamp (`sk:182`, `ht:410-413`, `ht:433-436`). Observed `HID ↑ 0xE1` at t+1501 (b2) after a 51 ms tap. The effective window after thumb-up is 1500 − hold time.
  - **A key buffered with the thumb by the hack (Δ = 6) also forces HOLD, at the flush (t+12).** MODEL (e)'s "(also for keys that were buffered with it and flushed together)" is confirmed (d).
  - **`moAndBuffer` release:** `layer 4 state 0` at thumb-up + 131 native (a, c, d). Then `Processing next queued behavior in 130ms`, so the queue stays parked to thumb-up + 262 native (T-18 territory; the start of the park is seen here).

## 📜 Spec (CLAIMS.md, verbatim)

> **T-17 `htls` hold-preferred** — R1 with pos 10 = `&htls 4 LSHIFT`. (a) `PRESS(2,2,30) PRESS(1,1,20) RELEASE(1,1,10) RELEASE(2,2,300)`: `decided hold (hold-preferred decision moment other-key-down)` at `[t+31]`, `layer 4 state 1` same ms, `kp_pressed LCTRL (0xE0)` same ms (htm hold-while-undecided). (b) `PRESS(2,2,50) RELEASE(2,2,300)`: `decided tap … key-up` at `t+51`, `kp_pressed 0xE1` (LSHIFT sticky). (c) hold 120 alone: `decided hold … timer` at `t+90`, i.e. `[t+91]`, not `t+102`. Purpose: E-99/E-100/E-108.

- **Claims exercised (E-claims.md):**
  - E-99: "`htls` thumb key is **hold-preferred**, resolves on interrupt or after 90ms".
  - E-100: "Hold (>= 90ms) activates the mod layer via `moAndBuffer`. Tap (< 90ms) activates a sticky mod via `skms`". CLAIMS marks it contradicted*: an interrupt before 90 is a hold.
  - E-108: "Mod layer becomes active at t=90 (tapping-term expiry), not at thumb press".
- **MODEL (e), Callum mods:** "`htls` (LH1, hold-preferred 90): held alone → `moAndBuffer↓` at `T + 90` (+δ; the 11 ms hack delay does not extend it) → `l_mod` ON; any other key DOWN while undecided → HOLD immediately (also for keys that were buffered with it and flushed together); released before 90 with no other key → TAP = `skms LSHIFT` (sticky 1500 ms, no ignore-modifiers)."
- **Checked against RIG.md.**
  - Coordinates OK: pos 10 = (2,2), pos 5 = (1,1), layer 4 pos 5 = `&htm LCTRL LCTRL`.
  - Keycodes OK: LCTRL 0xE0, LSHIFT 0xE1.
  - Mock times OK: the spec already uses the doubled first gap. (a) t = abs 31, pos 5 at abs 62 = t+31. (b) up at abs 102 = t+51. (c) up at abs 242 = t+121.
- **Log wording correction (cosmetic, not a deviation).** The firmware prints the status string, not the bare word "hold" (`ht:369-378`):
  - `decided hold-interrupt (hold-preferred decision moment other-key-down)`
  - `decided hold-timer (hold-preferred decision moment timer)`
  - `decided tap (hold-preferred decision moment key-up)`
- **Rig detail the spec did not account for.** R1's tier combos use `layers = <0 1 2 3 4 5>` (= `l_all`, per RIG.md), so S16 `<4 5>` and Q18 `<4 5 6>` are live on layer 4 for pos 5. The real config does the same for `l_all` combos on `l_mod` (26), e.g. `entr` `<13 14>` and `entr2` `<15 16>`.

## 🔩 Board translation (native +1 ms removed; δ ∈ (0.03, 1.05] ms per MODEL (a))

- **Hack flush / hold-tap creation:** native t+12 → board t+11+δ. Hack-only thumb, same as every hack-only key.
- **Interrupt decision (a)(a2)(a3)(a4):** event-driven, no timer. On the board it happens at the interrupting key's timestamp. Layer ON is in the same instant (queue idle → synchronous `&mo`).
  - LH1 and the `l_mod` home-row keys are all on the **left half (peripheral)**. Both are stamped on BLE receipt at the central, so the physical gap is quantised to the 7.5 ms anchor grid (MODEL (a) transport).
- **Mod down after an interrupt:**
  - No combo on the mod layer (LM4/LM3/LM2, RM2/RM3/RM4): the same instant as the decision.
  - LM1/RM1 on `l_mod` (`entr`/`entr2`, 16 ms, layers 0-31): the interrupting key's timestamp + 16 + δ (native +17). R1 pos 5 (Q18): +18 + δ (native +19).
  - Consequence for the page: Callum Shift on LM1/RM1 lags the layer by ~16 ms. If a letter is pressed on another key within that window, it is captured together with LM1 by the combo engine (it is not an `entr` member, so it flushes LM1 at its press). Not tested here.
- **Timer hold (c):** native t+91 → board **t+90+δ**, counted from the original press. The hack's 11 ms is not added.
- **Tap (b):** event-driven at key-up, identical on the board.
- **Sticky LSHIFT expiry (b2):** native t+1501 → board **thumb press + 1500 + δ**. Counted from the thumb press, so the post-release window is 1500 − hold time.
- **`moAndBuffer` release:** layer OFF at native thumb↑ + 131 → board thumb↑ + 130 + δ. The queue is then parked for another 130 (+δ) → thumb↑ + 260 + 2δ (T-18 measures the park).
- **(d) buffered-with-thumb:** on the board the flush runs at t+11+δ. The interrupt path goes through the hold-tap listener before the combo, so the Δ = 10 CANCELING strand (MODEL (d)) does not apply to the key the hold-tap captures. That is INFERRED; native runs Δ = 10 as a flush like Δ = 6.

### 📐 Corrected rule (replaces MODEL (e) `htls` interrupt clause; amends `htm`)

- **`htls` interrupt:** any other key DOWN while `htls` is undecided → `decided hold-interrupt` in the key's own ms (event-driven, no timer) → `&mo l_mod` runs synchronously → `layer 26 state 1` same ms. **Then the interrupting key is re-raised at the hold-tap listener (`ht:235`) and passes through the combo engine again with `l_mod` as the highest active layer (`combo.c:147`).** Combo R1 applies on `l_mod`: with ≥ 1 candidate whose `layers` include 26, the key is captured again and delivered only at **`ts_key + longest such candidate`** (+1 native, +δ board), `timeout_at` anchored at the key's original timestamp (`combo.c:155`); with 0 candidates it bubbles and is delivered in the decision ms.
- **`htm`:** the modifier is DOWN from the key's **delivery to the keymap** (`ht:523` hold-while-undecided), not from its press. Delivery = press + combo hold on the layer active at capture time.
- **Flo's `l_mod` (D-config §6.2 × `layers_C_extra.dtsi:42`):** of the eight `htm` keys, only **LM1 (`entr` <13 14>, 0–31, 16 ms)** and **RM1 (`entr2` <15 16>, 0–31, 16 ms)** have a combo live on 26 → their mod lands at **ts_key + 16 (+δ)** after a thumb interrupt (a4 observed LM1 at t+48 native; RM1 INFERRED from the table). LM4/LM3/LM2 and RM2/RM3/RM4 have none on 26 (`bspc`/`del`/`delWord`/`*Trans` skip 26, `capsWord`/`wispr`/hack are 0–24) → mod in the decision ms (a3 observed LM4 at t+31).
- **R1 rig numbers:** pos 5 (S16 16 / Q18 18 on 0–5) → t+31+18+1 = **t+50** (a); pos 3 (hack only, 0–3) → **t+31** (a2); pos 5 with S16/Q18 on 0–3 → **t+31** (fix).
- **Corrected T-17 (a) expectation:** `decided hold-interrupt (hold-preferred decision moment other-key-down)` at `[t+31]`, `layer 4 state 1` same ms, `kp_pressed 0xE0` at `[t+50]` with the rig's `l_all` tier masks (or `[t+31]` if the interrupting position has no candidate on layer 4).
