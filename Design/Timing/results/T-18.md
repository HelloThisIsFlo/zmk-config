# 🧪 T-18: Callum buffer (layer off at release+130, queue parked to release+260, shifted late key)

> **Status: complete.** 10 sub-cases built and ran on 2026-09-30:
> - 4 spec cases: (a), and variants (b), (c), (d).
> - 6 extras: (a2) B held, (b2) no-combo control, (b3) short tap, (e) shifted parked `&ak`, (f) the page's late scenario on Flo's real keymap, (g) real LM1 pressed late in the buffer.
> - Timestamped logs come from `zmk.exe --no-rt`. Second `run-test.sh` runs of (a) and (f) without auto-accept PASS against their snapshots. A re-run of the (a) exe gives the same md5, so the output is deterministic.
>
> **Verdict: `deviates`.** The MODEL mechanism holds exactly:
> - layer OFF at release + 130 (+1 native);
> - the behaviour queue is parked until release + 260 (+2 native);
> - a late `&ak` key is emitted at the end of the park, SHIFTED when a sticky Shift was armed.
>
> Four spec numbers or assertions do not match the log:
> - B is delivered at **t+273**, not t+274.
> - `kp_pressed 0x05` comes at **[t+383]**, not [t+382].
> - In the spec rig, the htm at 240 resolves as **F on base**, not LCTRL on layer 4.
> - The shifted F logs `explicit_mods 0x00`. The Shift is visible only as report modifiers `0x02`.

## 🎯 Result first

- ✅ **Layer 4 OFF at release + 131 native** in every R1 case: `[t+252]` with the thumb up at t+121. `l_mod` 26 OFF also comes at t+252 in R2 (f, g).
- ✅ **`Processing next queued behavior in 130ms` is logged twice.**
  - The first is at thumb-up (after the `&none` tap).
  - The second is at layer OFF (after `&mo` release).
- ✅ **The queue is parked until release + 262 native (= release + 260 + two 1 ms native timer offsets).**
  - Every `&ak` key delivered in the window emits its HID ↓ at exactly t+383: B in (a) (a2) (e), A in (f), T in (g).
  - In every case that is 100–122 ms after its keymap delivery.
- ❌ **B-key delivery is t+273, not t+274.**
  - The spec's own events release B 11 ms after the press (`PRESS(0,1,10)` → gap 11).
  - The key-up at t+273 flushes the combo one tick before the hack timer (t+274) would.
  - With B held 30 ms (a2), the hack timer delivers it at `[t+274]` (`timestamp=394` printed), matching the spec's number.
- ❌ **`kp_pressed 0x05` is at `[t+383]` (= release + 262), not `[t+382]` (= release + 261).**
  - The park is two chained `K_MSEC(130)` timers, and each one is +1 on native.
- ❌ **Spec rig (b): the htm on pos 5 pressed at t+240 does NOT resolve on layer 4.**
  - R1's `l_all` combos S16/Q18 capture pos 5 on layer 4 for 18 ms (+1).
  - It is delivered at t+259, after layer OFF at t+252. It resolves on base as `&kp F` → **F**, no LCTRL.
  - Controls confirm the spec's intent:
    - no combo at that position (b2) → LCTRL at t+240, on layer 4;
    - a 10 ms tap (b3) → the key-up flushes the combo at t+250 → LCTRL on layer 4.
- ⚠️ **"`kp_pressed 0x09` carries `explicit_mods 0x02`" is not what the log shows.**
  - `hid_listener_keycode_pressed` prints the *event's* own mods, which are `0x00` for `&kp F`.
  - The Shift is in the report instead: `zmk_hid_implicit_modifiers_press: Modifiers set to 0x02` in the same ms.
  - The Shift is released right after F↑. So F **is** shifted (d); only the log field is different.
- 🆕 **New facts:**
  - **Real keymap (g): Shift pressed late in the buffer becomes a delayed "t".**
    - LM1 (l_mod `&htm LSHIFT`, but in `entr` on l_mod) pressed at t+240 and held 30 is captured 16 ms (+1).
    - It is delivered at t+257, after `l_mod` OFF at t+252. It resolves on base as `&ak 19 T`, and T is parked to t+383.
    - The effective closing edge of the 130 ms buffer for LM1 is ≈ 16 ms earlier for a key held past its combo deadline.
  - **Raw `&kp` keys are not parked** (c, d): F is emitted at press + 19 (t+281), i.e. 102 ms *before* the queue resumes.
  - **The sticky Shift survives the whole park** (e, f).
    - It is consumed by the parked key's queued `kp ↓`.
    - It is released in the same ms as that key's queued `kp ↑` (t+394), before the `aksl` layer turns ON.

The MODEL's Callum mechanism holds; its rule is incomplete on one point (the closing edge for combo-captured keys), which the real keymap (g) makes relevant. Harness: nothing beyond the known +1 per relative timer (G §1/§5).

- **Corrected rule (MODEL addendum to "Callum mods"):** a key pressed inside the 130 ms buffer resolves on `l_mod` iff its DELIVERY to the keymap precedes layer OFF (release + 130 + δ₁). Delivery = min(own key-up, press + k + δ), k = the longest combo candidate live on `l_mod` for that position (0 when none). So the buffer's effective closing edge is **release + 130 − k** for a key still held at its deadline, and **release + 130** for a key released earlier or with no `l_mod` combo.
  - Real keymap: LM1/RM1 (Shift) sit in `entr`/`entr2` (`COMBO_ANY_ONE_HAND` → `l_all`, 16 ms) → the last ≈ 16 ms of the buffer turn a held Shift into the base `&ak` letter, as (g) shows ('t' at t+383). LM4/RM4 (Ctrl, no combo on `l_mod`) keep the full 130.
  - The same rule, applied to the AK layer ON edge, is MODEL "Visibility to the next key" (D+10). This is its OFF-edge twin.

### 4️⃣ (d) shifted F logs `explicit_mods 0x00`, not `0x02` → TEST, wrong log field

- `hid_listener.c:37-38` prints `ev->implicit_modifiers` and `ev->explicit_modifiers` of the keycode EVENT, i.e. modifiers baked into the binding (`LS(F)` style). `&kp F` carries none.
- A Shift held or sticky via `&kp LSHIFT` goes `hid.c:293-296` → `zmk_hid_register_mod` (`hid.c:52-59`) → `explicit_modifiers` bit + `SET_MODIFIERS` (`hid.c:42-46`, logs the REPORT byte). Every later `SET_MODIFIERS` logs it again: `zmk_hid_implicit_modifiers_press` (`hid.c:265-270`) at F↓.
- Evidence: `[00:00:00.200,000] … zmk_hid_register_mod: Modifiers set to 0x02` (Shift↓); `[00:00:00.381,000] … hid_listener_keycode_pressed: usage_page 0x07 keycode 0x09 implicit_mods 0x00 explicit_mods 0x00` + `[00:00:00.381,000] … zmk_hid_implicit_modifiers_press: Modifiers set to 0x02` (the report sent at F↓ = Shift + F); `[00:00:00.393,000] … hid_listener_keycode_released: usage_page 0x07 keycode 0xE1 …` → `zmk_hid_unregister_mod: Modifiers set to 0x00`.
- The spec's `skms` cannot be the source either: `skms` is `htls`'s TAP, and the thumb is HELD (`decided hold-timer` at t+91). On `l_mod` the Shift is `&htm LSHIFT LSHIFT` → `skm` (ignore-modifiers), mod DOWN from the press, count 2 → 1 at the tap, as MODEL's htm rule says.
- **Corrected expectation:** F↓ event line with `explicit_mods 0x00` AND `Modifiers set to 0x02` in the same ms; output "F". No re-run needed; (d), (e), (f) already show it.
